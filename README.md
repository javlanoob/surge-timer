# Surge Timer
Darkens every dose of surge potion in your inventory while the potion is on cooldown, with a timer on it showing when you can drink it again, in minutes and seconds or in game ticks. The potion gets its color back from the bottom up, or the top down, as the cooldown runs out.

- The timer follows the game's own surge potion cooldown, so it's right after logging in, and it's cleared when the game removes the cooldown, like on death or entering a raid.
- The cooldown pauses in some places, like between Fortis Colosseum and Inferno waves, between Theatre of Blood rooms and between Doom of Mokhaiotl delve levels. The timer stops with it.
