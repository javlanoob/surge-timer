# Surge Timer
Grays out every dose of surge potion in your inventory and bank while the potion is on cooldown, with a timer on it showing when you can drink it again.

- The timer follows the game's own surge potion cooldown, so it's right after logging in, and it's cleared when the game removes the cooldown, like on death or entering a raid.
- The cooldown pauses in some places, like between Fortis Colosseum and Inferno waves, between Theatre of Blood rooms and between Doom of Mokhaiotl delve levels. The timer stops with it, and turns yellow while it's paused.
- The timer and paused colors can be changed, and the bank can be left out.
